A short source-audio handle extends the practice recording beyond its picture cut for that dissolve; the practice audio out-point is recorded in `sync.json`. Both camera sources retain their **original unseparated sound**, including any backing or room musicians already recorded. Neither is described as an isolated guitar stem. A gentle **120 Hz, second-order high-pass** reduces competing kick/bass; there is no neural separation or replacement note.
