01 Raskys

Not just somewhere to drink.

Raskys is the venue I want to open: cocktails, wine, live music and the kind of service that makes people feel remembered. It doesn't exist yet. The plan does.

Where it starts

A cocktail and wine bar with a stage

Signature cocktails, carefully chosen wine, seasonal menus, live music, DJs, tastings and mixology classes, with seating that gets people talking. One day, somewhere warm and beachside.

Where it could go

A destination

A larger venue with a restaurant, several bars, live stages, a rooftop, boutique bowling, casino-style games and space for private events.

04 About

Good nights are my trade.

I've worked in hospitality for 10+ years, from pubs and restaurants in Suffolk to management roles on the south coast. These days I'm an assistant manager at a beachside venue in Hove. I know how to look after a room, and how to give people a good night.

The rest of my time goes on making things. Music as Raskyjack, games and interactive worlds, websites and software built with AI. Some ideas come from problems I’ve met at work; others simply from wanting to create something new. And when there’s time, I write.

Raskys is where it's all heading: a venue with good drinks, live music and the kind of service I'd want to receive myself.
