How your information is handled

Who receives it. Your statement is sent to Jack only. It is emailed to Jack's personal email address and a copy of the original submission is kept in Jack's private Google Drive, so the exact wording you submit is preserved.

How your identity is used. The privacy option you choose on the next screen determines how Jack is permitted to use identifying information from your statement. Confidential statements are clearly marked so that your identity is not provided to Rockwater or The HR Team without Jack asking you first.

Anonymous statements. If you choose anonymous, you won't be asked for your name or contact details and this form does not record your IP address. However, the details of what you describe, or files you upload, may still make you identifiable, and the website and email providers involved may keep their own standard technical logs.

Nothing else is collected. This page has no analytics, tracking or advertising. Nobody else who completes this form can see your answers, and you won't be shown anyone else's.

How long it's kept. Statements are kept only for as long as they're needed for Jack's appeal and any related HR process.

Changing your mind. After submitting, you'll be given a reference. You can use it to correct or withdraw your statement.

There is no pressure to complete this form. Only provide information you're comfortable sharing.
