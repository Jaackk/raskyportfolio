Rockwater Group Employee Handbook

April 2026 · Rockwater internal document · not reproduced here

Rockwater's handbook isn't published on this page. If you'd like to check it, please refer to your own copy or ask Rockwater. Sections that may be relevant include:

  • Disciplinary and Grievance Procedures, which contains the disciplinary procedure, a list of gross misconduct examples, and the appeals procedure
  • Employee Code of Conduct, which contains a separate list of gross misconduct examples
  • Staff Drinks
  • Alcohol and drugs
  • Anti-Harassment and Bullying Policy

Alcohol is mentioned in more than one of these sections, and the wording is not identical in each.
