The Novel Shnorkscape

Shnorkscape

In development

Shnorkscape is the forest of the book, built to be walked through. The current private build covers the first chapters: waking in the moss, hunger, the stream and the gouges, building a nest, the silent night and the chase, the scrap of hide, the Others — and the first offering.

Filmed in the forest

From the page to the world

Game images and films are captured from the current development build of Shnorkscape (0.5.0), framed and lit for this site — they are camera moves through the world, not gameplay recordings. The characters are still being refined against the book’s artwork, so they are not shown here yet.

Where it stands

Shnorkscape is being built alongside the book. The forest already has weather, day and night, water, wildlife and sound. Release details will be shared here when they exist.
