The work

In the Shadow of a Shnork — the novel’s text and illustrations, and the game — are original works. All rights reserved. Excerpts on this site are taken from the manuscript of 30 April 2026.

Game images were captured from the game’s development build. Its forest uses the Mega Stylized PBR Environment Pack by Piloto Studio, licensed to the project through the Unity Asset Store; it appears here only as rendered images.

Sound

  • “Memories Of Stone” by Scott Buckley — scottbuckley.com.au, licensed CC BY 4.0. Excerpt, looped and mixed with ambience.
  • “Giant Wolf Howl” by NaturesTemper — freesound.org, licensed CC BY 4.0. Filtered, echoed and faded for distance.
  • Foliage sounds: “Nature Sounds Pack” by Antoinemax (Antoine Goumain) — OpenGameArt, licensed CC BY 4.0. Trimmed, converted to mono, normalised.
  • “Park ambiences” by Thimras, “Rain (Ambient)” by Ove Melaa and “100 CC0 metal and wood SFX” by rubberduck — all via OpenGameArt under CC0. Credited with thanks.
  • Heartbeat, breath, creature voices, the predator, crickets, owl and the Pale Bloom tone were synthesised for the game.

Privacy

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking. It remembers three small preferences in your browser only — that you have visited, whether sound was on, and whether you prefer pages to the cinematic journey.
