IMPORTANT RELEASE GATES
1. Most selected screenshots are 1280×720. Steam requires 1920×1080 minimum in 16:9.
   Native screenshots are preserved rather than relabelled as compliant. Some
   promotional canvases are enlarged, which cannot restore unavailable detail.
2. All App Store designs use desktop QA imagery. Their RGB canvases have the
   checked pixel dimensions, but images must be replaced by genuine iPhone capture.
3. Native 4K current-build environment/character capture is needed for truly crisp
   large capsules, hero artwork and the ambitious visual benchmark in the brief.
   This handoff does not claim the existing QA imagery matches the fidelity of the
   AI reference scenes; it preserves the actual game's appearance.
4. Genuine two-player campaign footage is missing; a placeholder and exact shot plan
   are supplied. Do not replace it with AI companionship or invented interactions.
5. Confirm developer/publisher name, release information, price/purchase model,
   supported devices/languages, age rating, privacy/support URLs and system specs.
6. Final iOS minimum version, iPad support and icon/Xcode packaging remain unverified.
7. Owner visual approval and final current-platform submission review remain required.
