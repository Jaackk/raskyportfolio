ABOUT THIS GAME

The forest was here before you.

Its roots shape the paths. Its water disappears between stones. Somewhere beyond the familiar trees, something is waiting to be understood.

In The Shadow of a Shnork is an atmospheric fantasy adventure about finding your place in a world much larger than yourself. Explore as the Shnork: a small, expressive creature drawn onward by hunger, danger and an unfolding curiosity.

FOLLOW YOUR CURIOSITY

Walk among towering trunks, tangled roots and a richly planted forest floor. Trace the stream towards its waterfall. The way forward begins with noticing what is around you.

EVERY SHADOW HAS A STORY

Discover the Shnork’s world through encounters, story moments and field notes. Follow the first survival journey into the hunt, its aftermath and a meeting that brings another life closer to yours.

WONDER

Follow flowing water through the Home Forest. Pause over the small details beneath the canopy.

COMPANIONSHIP

A story encounter brings an unexpected companion. This image shows the AI companion, not multiplayer.

ADVENTURE TOGETHER

The development Beta supports private online co-op sessions. Invite another player into an expedition and face encounters together. Story progress and multiplayer visits remain distinct.

Co-op is evidenced in earlier Windows Beta tests. A genuine campaign-quality two-player capture is still required. Public matchmaking, host migration and cross-device support are not promised here.

RELEASE STATUSDesign review

No campaign image is labelled FINAL simply because it exported successfully.

What is confirmed

Release records identify commit 553f2d23008de72cbc118b4d3f71c9ed2e5e5a98 for build 33. Its exact archived Windows player passed a rendered Home/map/HUD smoke test. Earlier Beta evidence supports the first story arc, resource gathering, basic crafting, AI companionship and private Windows co-op.

The full Stag victory/reward/reload loop, physical iPhone acceptance and cross-device multiplayer are not accepted as complete by this audit. Fishing is deferred. Public matchmaking, host migration, Steam Cloud, controller support and localization are not inferred.

What the audit rejected

Reference fantasy landscapes, AI concept-region sheets, character experiments, asset-pack display scenes, test overlays, comparisons and obsolete green-goblin imagery were excluded from the storefront artwork. Older footage is confined to permanently labelled editorial drafts.

What needs a replacement

Most source stills are 1280 × 720. The Steam gallery preserves their native pixels rather than pretending an upscale is new detail; its release export needs 1920 × 1080 or better. Larger capsule canvases are clearly marked as resampled review artwork.

All six iPhone layouts need matching real device captures. Developer name, release date, price, rating, privacy declarations, compatibility, languages and requirements remain unconfirmed. Co-op has a reserved capture slot rather than a fabricated image.
