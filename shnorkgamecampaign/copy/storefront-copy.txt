STEAM ABOUT THIS GAME / FULL DESCRIPTION
A small life. An ancient world.

The forest was here before you. Its roots shape the paths. Its water disappears between stones. Somewhere beyond the familiar trees, something is waiting to be understood.

In The Shadow of a Shnork is an atmospheric fantasy adventure about finding your place in a world much larger than yourself. Explore as the Shnork: a small, expressive creature drawn onward by hunger, danger and an unfolding curiosity.

FOLLOW YOUR CURIOSITY
Walk among towering trunks, tangled roots and a richly planted forest floor. Trace the stream towards its waterfall. Pause over the small details. The way forward begins with noticing what is around you.

EVERY SHADOW HAS A STORY
Discover the Shnork’s world through encounters, story moments and field notes. Follow the first survival journey into the hunt, its aftermath and a meeting that brings another life closer to yours. Some discoveries change the way familiar places feel.

WONDER HAS A WILD SIDE
There is movement beneath the canopy, and not every encounter is gentle. Explore, gather, craft and face danger with the tools you have. Learn when to approach, when to strike and when to move.

A LITTLE LESS ALONE
Companionship is part of the Shnork’s story. The development Beta also supports private online co-op sessions: invite another player into an expedition and face encounters together. Story progress and multiplayer visits remain distinct.

DEVELOPMENT NOTE
The game is in active development. This presentation is grounded in existing Windows Beta gameplay. The current validated Windows package is Beta 0.8.8 / build 33; its narrow release validation did not repeat the full earlier story and multiplayer suite. World Beyond imagery belongs only to the separate, explicitly labelled development-preview appendix. Release date, price, final platform features, system requirements and language support have not been announced here.
