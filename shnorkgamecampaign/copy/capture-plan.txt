IN THE SHADOW OF A SHNORK — CAPTURE & REPLACEMENT PLAN
Prepared 13 October 2026. No game build or capture session was launched for this campaign.

SAFE PROCESS
Wait for the active development/storage jobs to finish. Use a separate copy of the validated Windows package, outside all Unity worktrees. Confirm the exact version and content with the owner. Use a disposable named QA profile and an isolated user-data/save directory through an existing supported mechanism; if isolation cannot be guaranteed, stop before launching. Do not overwrite canonical saves or user preferences. Do not rebuild Unity, merge World Beyond, modify scenes, inject assets or change multiplayer. Use existing, supported camera/UI controls only. Preserve an unedited master with build and capture notes.

WINDOWS CAPTURE
Target 3840×2160 native, highest stable approved graphics setting, 30 or 60 fps. No artificial frame generation or AI image enhancement. Capture 10–15 seconds before and after each usable moment. Keep characters grounded and anatomically unchanged. Avoid debug HUD, test subtitles, desktop chrome, loading, clipping or broken lighting. Preserve a gameplay HUD version for screenshot review; hide UI only through an existing approved in-game toggle. Do not disguise technical defects with retouching.

Priorities for an 8–10-image Steam gallery:
01 Home Forest wide: current build, player in lower third, clean trunk/fern depth, native 4K.
02 Existing waterfall: full Shnork on one bank, falls and connected stream visible. Current grounding fixes required.
03 River walk: retain bank plants and reflections; avoid foreground occlusion.
04 Woodland exploration: a different route and camera direction from spawn; expressive movement, not idle duplicates.
05 Creature encounter: approved current wildlife or predator at a readable distance. Verify species and implementation from the build.
06 Story: a genuine first-offering or field-note moment without test subtitles. Avoid treating the AI companion as multiplayer.
07 Two-player private co-op: two separately controlled current clients, visible shared interaction or accepted Stalker encounter, both players identifiable. Remove session codes and private identifiers from capture through safe framing.
08 Danger: a readable, accepted combat phase. Do not advertise a full Rot Stag victory until its reward/reload acceptance passes.
09 Environmental detail: ferns, fungus, water and roots with no artificial detail added.
10 Mysterious location: approved production geography, not an unfinished World Beyond scene.

STEAM RULES
Export at least five authentic gameplay screenshots at 1920×1080 minimum, 16:9. Do not add promotional copy or capsule art to the screenshot gallery. Current native 720p files are review selections, not upload-ready screenshots. A conventional upscale does not create the missing detail. Capsule artwork may use crops, layout and restrained grading; retain correct game identity and use only game-world elements. No review scores, discount text, CTAs or taglines on standard capsules. Review small capsule at 120×45 and hero/logo positioning with the current Valve template.

IPHONE CAPTURE
First pass physical-device install, launch, controls, readable UI, representative performance and the advertised story/feature paths. Current iOS export validation was on Windows only. Capture actual iPhone screen recordings and stills using the supported orientation. Do not substitute Windows QA footage. Use matching compositions to replace the six poster designs. Export opaque RGB PNG/JPEG, 1–10 per device slot. Prepared portrait canvases: 1206×2622 medium and 1320×2868 large. Recompose rather than stretch game imagery.
