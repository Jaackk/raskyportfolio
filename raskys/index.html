Raskys · a venue in the making

A bar people remember.

Raskys is the cocktail, wine, music and social venue I'm planning. This page is the thinking so far: the idea, the menu and the plan. Nothing here has been built yet, and every image of a venue is concept work.

Read the business plan (PDF) Talk to me about Raskys

01 The idea

Somewhere people go to connect, celebrate and remember.

Raskys is not just somewhere to drink. It starts as a cocktail and wine bar with live music and events, built around warm, personal service and a brand you'd recognise from across the street.

The first version of the idea was a Brighton-style coastal bar. It isn't tied to one city: the plan suits anywhere coastal, leisure-led or busy at night, wherever the right site turns up.

Premium without being cold. Exciting and social, but still somewhere you can relax.

  • Young professionals

    After-work drinks, dates, celebrations and evenings out somewhere with some style.

  • Locals

    A reliable, good-quality bar with personality to come back to every week.

  • Visitors

    Tourists looking for somewhere distinctive in a lively part of town.

  • Students & younger guests

    Events, offers and premium drinks that still feel affordable.

  • Music & events crowds

    People who come for live music, DJs, themed nights and tastings.

03 Service

The drinks are only half of it.

I've spent my working life on bar and restaurant floors. The bit guests remember is usually how they were looked after.

  1. Know the menu

    Staff who can recommend a drink properly and explain what's in it.

  2. Make it personal

    Remember regulars, their names and what they like.

  3. Keep it moving

    Efficient service that doesn't feel rushed, even when it's busy.

  4. Give reasons to stay

    Music, seasonal events and a room designed for people to linger.

04 Events

A calendar, not just a bar.

Events are how people hear about a place and why they come back. The plan starts with:

  • Live music

    Bands and solo sets, plus DJs later in the evening.

  • Mixology classes

    Learn to make the house drinks.

  • Tastings

    Seasonal cocktails and wines.

  • Themed & student nights

    Regular nights with a reason to show up.

  • Charity & community

    Nights that tie the bar to the area around it.

  • Private hire

    Birthdays, celebrations and company events.

05 Where it could go

From a bar to a destination.

Once the bar works, the long-term version is bigger: a restaurant, several bars, live music stages, a rooftop, boutique bowling, casino-style games and private event space, all under the same name and the same standard of service.
