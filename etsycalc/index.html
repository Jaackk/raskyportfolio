How the calculator works

For each sale it adds up the fees Etsy takes from a UK seller, works out the VAT Etsy adds to those fees, then subtracts your own costs from what is left. All the figures below come from Etsy's own pages and were last checked on 3 November 2026. Etsy can change them at any time, so treat the results as estimates.

    Questions sellers ask

    Which fees can I avoid?

    Offsite Ads (if your shop is under $10,000 of sales over 365 days), Etsy Ads and currency conversion are optional or conditional. The transaction fee, payment processing, regulatory operating fee and listing fee apply to normal UK sales. Read the full UK fees guide.

    Do I pay VAT on Etsy's fees if I'm not VAT registered?

    Etsy says it may charge VAT on its seller fees depending on your business status and location. Non-registered sellers cannot reclaim it, so this calculator counts it as a cost. VAT on Etsy fees explained.

    How do I know what price to charge?

    Use the "Find the right price" panel. It works backwards from your costs and fee options to the item price that reaches your target margin or profit. How to price handmade products.

    Why is the listing fee not a fixed number of pounds?

    Etsy charges 0.20 USD and converts it to pounds, so the pound amount moves with the exchange rate. Set the rate under "Edit Etsy's rates".

    This calculator gives estimates, not tax, legal or financial advice. Check your own Etsy payment account for the exact amounts.
