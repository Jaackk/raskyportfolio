Etsy fees UK: every seller fee explained (2026)

Updated 3 November 2026. Figures checked against Etsy's own pages.

Etsy takes several fees from every UK sale. Some are percentages, some are flat amounts, and a few only apply in particular situations. This guide lists each one, what it is charged on and what it costs on a typical order.

Quick answer. On a normal UK sale with no Offsite Ads, Etsy's fees come to about 14.9% of the order for a seller who is not VAT registered, or 12.4% for a VAT-registered seller who reclaims VAT on fees. That is on a £25.00 order (£20.00 item plus £5.00 postage).

Transaction fee: 6.5%

Etsy charges 6.5% of the price you display plus what you charge for postage and gift wrapping. If you charge for personalisation, that amount is added to the price first. For sellers outside the US the fee also applies to any tax included in your listing price. Because postage is included, offering "free postage" and building the cost into the item price does not avoid the fee on that money.

Payment processing: 4% + £0.20

For a UK bank account, Etsy Payments charges 4% of the total sale plus £0.20 per order. The percentage applies to the whole amount the buyer pays for goods and postage. The flat 20p means small orders lose a bigger share than large ones.

Regulatory operating fee: 0.48%

Sellers in certain countries pay a regulatory operating fee on each sale. Etsy announced that the UK rate increased from 0.32% to 0.48% from 22 June 2026. It is charged on the item price plus postage, in addition to the other fees. Many online calculators were last updated before that change and still use the older figure.

Listing fee: 0.20 USD

Each listing costs 0.20 USD to publish and lasts four months. If it is still listed after four months, automatic renewal charges another 0.20 USD unless you switch renewal to manual. A listing with several items in stock is renewed at 0.20 USD each time one sells. Etsy bills in dollars and converts to pounds at the market rate, so the pound amount changes slightly with the exchange rate. This site's calculator uses a rate you can edit. Etsy does not publish a fixed pound figure, so treat any such figure as an estimate.

Offsite Ads: 15% or 12%, only when they bring the sale

If a buyer clicks an advert Etsy bought on another site and orders from your shop within 30 days, Etsy charges an advertising fee on that order. The rate is 15%, or 12% for shops that have passed 10,000 USD of sales in a 365-day period. The fee is capped at 100 USD per order. Smaller shops can opt out. See the full Offsite Ads guide.

Etsy Ads

Etsy Ads are optional and work on a daily budget. Etsy's help pages give a minimum daily budget of 1 USD. Spend is charged as clicks happen, so the cost per sale varies. In the calculator you can enter your average ad spend per sale to see its effect on profit.

Currency conversion: 2.5%

If you list in a currency other than the one in your Etsy Payments account, Etsy charges 2.5% on the sale amount to send the funds to your account. Listing in the same currency as your payment account avoids it.

VAT on Etsy's fees

Etsy's published fees exclude VAT. Depending on your business status and location, Etsy may add VAT to the fees it charges you. If you are not VAT registered you cannot reclaim it, so it is a real cost. If you are VAT registered you can normally reclaim it. VAT on Etsy fees explained.

What the fees cost on a real order

Here are the fees on a £25.00 order (£20.00 item plus £5.00 postage) for a seller who is not VAT registered, with no Offsite Ads. Each line is rounded to the penny, so the lines can differ from the total by a penny.

FeeAmount
Transaction fee (6.5%)£1.63
Payment processing (4% + £0.20)£1.20
Regulatory operating fee (0.48%)£0.12
Listing fee (0.20 USD at 0.75)£0.15
VAT on those fees (20%)£0.62
Total fees£3.71 (14.9% of the order)

The share Etsy takes is lower on bigger orders because the flat amounts spread over more money:

Order (item + postage)Total feesShare of order
£10.00 + £3.00£2.1316.4%
£20.00 + £4.00£3.5814.9%
£50.00 + £5.00£7.6713.9%

All of these include VAT on fees and the listing fee, and assume no Offsite Ads. A VAT-registered seller who reclaims VAT on fees pays £3.10 on the £25.00 order.

A quick rule of thumb

With the default rates and a seller who is not VAT registered, Etsy keeps roughly 13.2% of the order plus about 42p. For a VAT-registered seller it is about 11% plus 35p. This ignores Offsite Ads, Etsy Ads and currency conversion. Use the calculator for a precise figure.
