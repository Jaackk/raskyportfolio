Privacy and cookies

This policy covers the whole of raskyjack.com, including the Etsy Profit Calculator, the guides, and the music, design, software and game pages. It explains what happens to your information when you use the site. It was last updated on 3 November 2026.

Who runs this site

The site is run by Jack Ormondroyd (known online as Rasky), who is responsible for how personal data is used on it. Contact: hello@raskyjack.com. The site is not affiliated with Etsy, Inc.

Information you give me

The public pages have no accounts, sign-up forms or comment boxes. If you email hello@raskyjack.com, I receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. The address is a forwarding address provided by ImprovMX, so your message passes through that service on its way to my inbox. I keep emails for as long as they are useful and do not sell or share them.

Information collected automatically

The site is hosted by Netlify. As with any website, your IP address and standard request details (such as the page you asked for and your browser type) reach Netlify's servers so pages can be delivered and the service kept secure.

Most pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google to download them and Google receives your IP address. The Music page embeds players from Spotify and Apple Music, and some pages link out to services such as YouTube, SoundCloud, Instagram and TikTok. Those companies run their own services and may set their own cookies, especially once you press play or follow a link. A few project pages load code libraries from public content networks (for example the Motion Desk page loads Tailwind CSS). Each of these providers has its own privacy policy.

Cookies and things stored on your device

This site does not set cookies of its own. Some pages save settings or your own entries in your browser's local storage so they work next time you visit. Examples are the fee settings on the Etsy calculator, the light or dark choice on the Shnork game, notes in the Perfect Host demo, and your accept or reject choice if adverts are ever switched on. This stays on your device. You can clear it in your browser settings, and the calculator's "Reset everything" button clears its settings.

The Etsy Profit Calculator

Everything you type into the calculator is processed in your browser. It is not sent to a server and is not stored by this site. If you press "Copy link", the numbers are placed in the link you copy, in the part after the "#", which browsers do not send to a server.

Analytics

The Etsy calculator, its guides and this page use Umami (Umami Cloud) to understand how many people visit, where they come from and which pages and tools are used. Other pages on the site do not use analytics. Umami says its tracker does not use cookies, and this site stores nothing on your device for analytics.

For each page view Umami records the page address, the referring site, your browser and operating system, device type, screen size and approximate location (country or region). Your browser's request necessarily reaches Umami's servers; Umami's documentation lists the data it collects. The calculator also sends a few named events when you use a feature, for example "calculator used", "price helper used", "compare used", "copy summary", "copy link" and "export CSV". These events do not include the numbers you type in.

The part of a page address after a "#" or "?" is not sent, so a shared calculator link does not leak its figures. If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal, analytics does not run. Umami also collects page speed measurements (Core Web Vitals) so the site can be made faster. The data is used only for these statistics and is not combined with advertising data.

Advertising

At the time of writing no adverts are shown and no advertising scripts load anywhere on the site. This site is applying to show adverts from Google AdSense on the Etsy calculator pages. If they are switched on, visitors in the UK and the European Economic Area will be asked for consent first, either by this site's own accept or reject banner or by Google's consent message, which follows the industry TCF standard. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show, personalise and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, according to your choice. Depending on that choice you may see no adverts or adverts that are not personalised.

You will be able to change your choice at any time with the "Ad choices" link in the page footer. Declining does not affect the calculator. This page will be updated when adverts go live. You can learn more at Google's advertising technologies page.

Private tools

A small number of pages on this domain are private tools used by invited people, for example an events pre-order system and a staff questionnaire. They are not linked from the public site and are not covered by the public pages above. Anyone asked to use one is told what it collects and why when they are given access.

Legal basis and how long data is kept

Hosting logs and the small amount of technical data above are used because it is necessary to run and secure the site and to understand how it is used (legitimate interests). Emails are kept only as long as needed to deal with them. Consent is the basis for any advertising cookies, and you can withdraw it at any time.

Links to other sites

Pages here link to Etsy, GOV.UK, music services and other sites. They have their own privacy policies. Any link that earns this site a commission will be clearly labelled.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal data is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, object to its use, and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Email hello@raskyjack.com to make a request.

Changes

If this page changes materially, for example when advertising is switched on or a new service is added, the date at the top will be updated.
