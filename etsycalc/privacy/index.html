Privacy and cookies

Updated 3 November 2026. Figures checked against Etsy's own pages.

Who runs this site

This calculator is run by Jack Ormondroyd as part of raskyjack.com. It is a free tool and is not affiliated with Etsy, Inc.

What you type into the calculator

Everything you enter in the calculator is processed in your browser. It is not sent to a server and is not stored by this site. If you press "Copy link", the numbers are placed in the link you copy, in the part after the "#", which browsers do not send to the server.

What is stored on your device

The calculator saves your fee settings (for example your VAT status and edited rates) in your browser's local storage so you do not have to re-enter them. This stays on your device. "Reset everything" clears it. If advertising is switched on, your accept or reject choice is stored the same way.

Hosting and logs

The site is hosted by Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify and its network handle your IP address and standard request information in order to deliver pages and keep the service secure.

Analytics

This site uses Umami (Umami Cloud) to understand how many people visit, where they come from and which pages and tools are used. Umami says its tracker does not use cookies, and this site stores nothing on your device for analytics.

For each page view Umami records the page address, the referring site, your browser and operating system, device type, screen size and approximate location (country or region). Your browser's request necessarily reaches Umami's servers; Umami's documentation lists the data it collects. This site also sends a few named events when you use a feature, for example "calculator used", "price helper used", "compare used", "copy summary", "copy link" and "export CSV". These events do not include the numbers you type in.

The part of a page address after a "#" or "?" is not sent, so a shared calculator link does not leak its figures. If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal, analytics does not run. Umami also collects page speed measurements (Core Web Vitals) so the site can be made faster. The data is used only for these statistics and is not combined with advertising data.

Advertising

At the time of writing no adverts are shown and no advertising scripts load. If adverts are added, they will be provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show and measure ads. Before any of that loads you will be asked to accept or reject, and you can change your choice at any time with the "Ad choices" link in the page footer. Rejecting does not affect the calculator. You can also learn more at Google's advertising technologies page.

Links to other sites

Pages here link to Etsy, GOV.UK and other sites. They have their own privacy policies. Any link that earns this site a commission will be clearly labelled.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal data is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Because this site does not collect personal data from the calculator, there is usually nothing to access. To raise a request, use the contact details on raskyjack.com.

Changes

If this page changes materially, for example when analytics or advertising is switched on, the date at the top will be updated.
