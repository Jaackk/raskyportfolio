## Context and limits

- **No Friday–Sunday restriction identified (ANALYSIS)**: No Friday–Sunday staff-drink restriction was identified in the four supplied documents. This does not establish that no separate workplace policy, instruction or communicated rule exists. Ask where the restriction is documented and how it was communicated.
- **Two alcohol examples (ANALYSIS)**: The disciplinary procedure example includes possession or consumption; the Employee Code of Conduct example begins with consumption. Both include wording about possible effects on work performance or other employees. Actual harm is not stated as a prerequisite.
- **40 hours in both documents (ANALYSIS)**: Conditions of Employment already states an average of 40 hours per week. The September variation describes an hour increase to 40 per week. The intervening contractual or working position is not established by these files.
- **Template text remains in handbook (DOCUMENTED FACT)**: The supplied handbook includes unfilled template markers, optional text and alternative clauses. These are retained in the extracted source. Where relevant, establish which provision applies; do not treat a template marker alone as invalidating the policy.
- **Policy and benefits status (ANALYSIS)**: Benefits and the disciplinary procedure are expressly described as non-contractual. The post-shift drink is conditional on duty-manager discretion and the shift having ended. The variation preserves other terms; it does not turn this benefit into an unconditional entitlement.
- **ACAS statement context (DOCUMENTED FACT)**: The statement that the policy fully incorporates the ACAS code appears under Grievance and Dispute Policy, after a non-contractual-status paragraph. It should not be described as general contractual incorporation of all ACAS guidance.
